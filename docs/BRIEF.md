## Submission requirements (transcribed)

1. Working demo: a real iMessage conversation with your agent, end to end (message in, web action, useful result back in iMessage).
2. Uses Photon Spectrum for the iMessage layer and at least one TinyFish endpoint (Search, Fetch, or Agent) for the web layer, both doing real work.
3. Works for different requests, not a single scripted conversation.
4. Short explanation of what you built, who it's for, and how Photon and TinyFish are each used.
5. A 60-second demo posted on X and LinkedIn, tagging Photon and TinyFish.

## Approval criteria (transcribed)

1. Useful: it solves a real moment in a student's day, and someone would text it again tomorrow.
2. Uses at least one TinyFish endpoint meaningfully: it actually searches, reads, or acts on live websites, with no canned answers.
3. Photon and TinyFish work together end to end: message in, real multi-step web work, a useful result back in iMessage.
4. Remembers context and texts at the right moment; otherwise it stays quiet.
5. Robust and trustworthy: handles edge cases and failures, asks before anything is booked, bought, or sent, and never fakes a source.
6. Crafted: cohesive, responsive, and tasteful in iMessage.
7. Demo: the 60-second video is clear and convincing, showing the moment, the web work, and the payoff.

Scoring: 1 endpoint 50 points; 2 endpoints 100 points; 3+ endpoints 200 points.
Endpoints must contribute meaningfully; padding does not qualify.

challenge month, on top of usual TinyFish rewards. No cash amount stated here.
